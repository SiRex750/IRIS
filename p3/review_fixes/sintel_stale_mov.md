# Sintel STALE_MOV: stale share of moving cells (review follow-up B)

**Exploratory reporting, not a new verdict.** STALE_MOV is the confirmatory CCTV test's metric (pre-registration d62753f), applied after the fact to the saved Sintel sweep. The '> 0.01 in a sequence' rule is borrowed from that CCTV pre-registration and was not pre-registered for Sintel. Generated by `p3/review_fixes/b_sintel_stale_mov.py`.

- STALE_MOV = stale cells / moving cells. Stale cells (valid, reused at tau = 1 px, |GT| > 2 px) are `stale_cells` from p3/sweep/results.csv, final pass, tau = 1. Moving cells = valid cells (GT cover >= 0.5) with |GT| > 2 px, counted from the Sintel GT (lib/gt_grid.py) over the same frames as the row's frame group (bf 0 arms: `d1` P-frames; B arms: `B_naive`, i.e. the B-frames with the unscaled vector, as in the CCTV test).
- Check: valid cells recounted from GT over the same frames equal results.csv `valid_cells` on 506/506 rows. `valid_cells` and `stale_cells` of every row used are identical in p3/sweep_rerun/results.csv: yes.
- 23 sequences.
